What happens to my data?

Field name, crop history, priorities, and soil notes are not saved by this prototype. Map tiles reveal the viewed area to OpenStreetMap, and chosen coordinates are sent in live data requests; network providers or a future host may keep request logs. No account or farmer database is used. See the data-flow inventory.
