# Documentation and research instructions

These instructions apply inside `docs/` and supplement the root `AGENTS.md`.

## Purpose

Documentation must help the team make, verify, or explain a decision. Avoid filler, repeated project introductions, and unsupported claims.

## Required evidence pattern

For each scientific or user-needs claim, capture:

- Claim in the team's words.
- Exact result or a short compliant quotation.
- Source title, author/organization, year, direct URL or DOI.
- Page, table, figure, or section.
- Region, crop/system, soil/climate, and study period.
- Study type and sample size when relevant.
- Limitations and conflicting evidence.
- Confidence: `high`, `medium`, or `low`, with a reason.
- Allowed use: `app-rule`, `context-only`, `presentation-only`, or `not-usable`.

Do not treat a general paper as proof for a precise local numerical rule.

## Writing standard

- Lead with findings and decisions.
- Distinguish `Confirmed`, `Proposed`, `Unknown`, and `Future work`.
- Use tables for evidence matrices, not for decorative prose.
- Keep figures traceable to their source and reuse permission.
- Never convert the absence of evidence into evidence of no effect.
- Direct farmer interviews are not currently available; use published surveys/case studies and state this limitation.

## Maintenance

- `STATUS.md` is the current truth, not a historical diary.
- `DECISIONS.md` is append-only except for correcting errors; mark superseded decisions.
- `PROJECT_PLAN.md` holds sequence and acceptance gates.
- `COMPETITION_SCORECARD.md` is the pre-submission audit.
- `TEAM_RESEARCH_TASKS.md` contains the three non-technical research assignments.
