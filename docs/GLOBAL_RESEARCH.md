# Worldwide research catalog: scope audit

## Confirmed

The Research view now distinguishes worldwide observation/reference sources from two non-Iowa regional examples and the Central Iowa pilot. Every record in `data/global-research.json` is `context-only` and `research-only`. These records help frame questions; they never activate a crop trait, rotation rule, yield estimate, or field-suitability verdict. The claims below were checked against the linked primary source pages on 2026-10-12. A source may describe global coverage without proving that its values represent an individual field.

Sample size not applicable. | A valid point request can provide historical daily temperature and precipitation context. | Grid resolution and source products vary; missing readings and local weather differences matter. ## Unknown and next validation

- No agronomist has reviewed or approved these records for decision rules.
- This catalog is not a systematic worldwide literature review. The two regional examples are illustrative, not representative of all farming systems.
- A global soil service, region-specific crop traits and calendars, farmer validation, and locally reviewed rules are still required before crop comparisons can expand beyond Central Iowa.
