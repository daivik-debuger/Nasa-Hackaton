# Research evidence standard

## Preferred source order

1. Current official dataset documentation.
2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
3. Peer-reviewed region/crop-specific studies.
4. Government and university-extension publications.
5. Published farmer surveys and case studies.

Marketing pages, search snippets, AI answers, and unsourced summaries are discovery aids only.

## Required evidence columns

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Claim ID | Stable identifier, such as `ROT-WATER-001` |
| Claim | Narrow statement in team language |
| Result | Exact reported result or short compliant quotation |
| Source | Title, author/organization, year, DOI/direct URL |
| Location | Page, table, figure, or section |
| Applicability | Region, crops, soil/climate, management, period |
| Study quality | Type, duration, sample size, controls if relevant |
| Limitations | What the evidence does not establish |
| Conflicts | Supporting or disagreeing evidence |
| Confidence | High/medium/low plus reason |
| Allowed use | App rule, context, presentation, or not usable |
| Review | Reviewer and date |

## Review questions

- Does the source support this exact claim?
- Are units, thresholds, and direction copied correctly?
- Is the evidence applicable to the pilot region?
- Are short-term results being presented as long-term effects?
- Are correlation and causation separated?
- Is a mapped/model value being presented as a measurement?
- Is a negative or null result being omitted?
- Would the claim remain honest when shortened for the interface?

## Literature limitations

Direct farmer interviews are not currently planned because of time. Use published surveys to identify needs and explicitly list direct user validation as future work. Do not invent personas, quotations, or interview findings.
